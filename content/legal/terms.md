## 3. How we check listings

Every listing shows one of these:

- **Visited:** our Cebu team has seen the provider in person, checked their permits and licences, and taken photos.
- **Called:** we have spoken to the provider by phone and checked their permits and licences online.
- **Listed:** the provider added the listing and we have not checked it yet.

A check reflects what we saw or were told **on the date shown**. Checks are due again after 6 months, and the badge shows as lapsed if we haven't re-checked by then. If a family reports a problem, we take the badge down and show the listing as "Under review" until we've looked into it.

Prices, staff, services and licences can change quickly. We take reasonable care, but we cannot guarantee that a listing is complete or current, or that a provider will honour what it told us.

**Please make your own checks before hiring anyone.** Confirm prices and details with the provider, and where you can, visit in person or arrange a video call before you commit.
